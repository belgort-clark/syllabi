The rules that actually matter

  • Deadlines. Weekly work is due Sunday 11:59 PM and stays open three more days, to Wed 11:59 PM, at full credit — no penalty, no permission needed. Canvas will stamp it “late.” Ignore the label. In-class work and the final project are outside that window.
  • AI. The CS50 Duck (cs50.ai) is always allowed. No other AI may write, complete, or suggest code you submit; using one to explain a concept or an error message is fine — cite it in a comment. The standard: you must be able to write it again and explain it line by line, unaided. You’ll code in cs50.dev, which has AI autocomplete already turned off.
  • In-class, on paper. A short quiz every module (lowest dropped — that is the make-up), the midterm Wed, Oct 28, and the final exam Mon, Nov 30. None has a three-day window. Need DAC testing? Set up both exams in week one; book Nov 30 by Fri, Nov 20.
  • Participation is 25% — graded on effort, not correctness. Show your reasoning and a wrong answer still earns full credit.
  • Attendance. Come to both sessions. Miss one? Message me on Slack and I’ll give you an equivalent for the in-class work.
